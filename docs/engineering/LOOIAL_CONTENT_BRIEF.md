# Looial — content brief (from [looial.com](https://looial.com))

Use this for social, ads, and the content calendar. **Audience is merchants** (cafés, salons, shops, restaurants), not shoppers buying Looial as a product.

## One-liner

**Digital loyalty that lives in WhatsApp** — no app store download; Apple/Google Wallet and PWA are optional backups.

## What it does

| For merchants | For their customers |
|---------------|---------------------|
| Sign up, set stamp threshold + reward (~5 min) | Scan QR at counter → WhatsApp opens → enrolled |
| Print static QR at counter | Stamp progress updates in WhatsApp |
| Find customer by phone, 4-digit PIN, or QR; one-tap visit | Optional Wallet pass or PWA on home screen |
| Referrals, automations, analytics (by plan) | One Looial card can work across merchants on the platform |

## Problem → solution (site copy)

- **Problem:** First-time visitors often don’t return; paper cards get lost; loyalty apps aren’t installed; chain-grade POS is too heavy for local SMBs.
- **Solution:** WhatsApp-first card, merchant-controlled rewards, affordable tooling **built for African SMBs**, priced in local currency (FX on pricing page).

## Product pillars (feature pages)

1. **WhatsApp digital loyalty** — enrol in under ~30 seconds.
2. **Mobile Wallet & PWA** — backup, not the primary story.
3. **Referrals** — personal link; friend joins, both benefit.
4. **Automations** — win-back, welcome, near-reward (less daily manual work).
5. **Analytics** — who returns, who’s drifting, what rewards work.

**Stamp-adjacent actions** (marketing carousel on home): repeat visits, reviews, social engagement, questionnaire — loyalty tied to revenue actions, not only “visit counted.”

## Proof points (homepage)

- +30% repeat customers (marketing stat)
- 5 min setup
- 0 customer apps to download
- Testimonials: bookstore Abidjan, café Lagos, restaurant Accra

## Content do / don’t

| Do | Don’t |
|----|--------|
| Speak to **owners & staff** at local businesses | Lifestyle DTC, beauty “edits,” SKUs, shipping cutoffs for Looial-as-retailer |
| Show **counter, QR, WhatsApp notification, stamp progress** | Imply customers must download a Looial app store app |
| Mention **referrals, automations, analytics** on rotation | Position as chain POS replacement |
| Use **Jamhuri / local currency / African SMB** where relevant | Generic US-only “Small Business Saturday” without merchant angle |
